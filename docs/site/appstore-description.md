# App Store Description — Places in East Africa

**Plan unforgettable East Africa trips in seconds.**

Places in East Africa is your AI travel concierge for Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda and beyond. Tell it who's going, how long, and what you're hoping for — and get a day-by-day itinerary grounded in real places, with real prices and practical tips. No generic filler.

**What you can do**
• Generate complete, day-by-day itineraries with an AI concierge
• Explore real destinations and experiences — safaris, coastlines, cities, culture
• Plan multi-country routes across the region
• Save trips and bookmark the places you love
• Browse an interactive map of the region

**Go Pro**
Unlock the full experience with Places Pro:

• "Optimize my day" — smarter routing to cut backtracking
• A fresh batch of tokens every month
Choose weekly, monthly, or annual — or unlock Lifetime once and keep it forever.
